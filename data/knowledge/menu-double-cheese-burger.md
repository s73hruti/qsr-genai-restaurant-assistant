# Double Cheese Burger

## Description
Two beef patties, two slices of melted cheese and pickles.

## Price and availability
The Double Cheese Burger costs $6.20. Category: burger. Available all day during opening hours.

## Allergens and diet
Allergens: gluten, milk. Contains meat, fish or dairy ingredients. Tags: beef, cheese.

## Nutrition
Approximately 740 kcal per serving.
