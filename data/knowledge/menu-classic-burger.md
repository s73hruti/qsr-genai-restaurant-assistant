# Classic Burger

## Description
Grilled beef patty with pickles, lettuce and tomato on a toasted bun.

## Price and availability
The Classic Burger costs $4.50. Category: burger. Available all day during opening hours.

## Allergens and diet
Allergens: gluten. Contains meat, fish or dairy ingredients. Tags: beef, kids.

## Nutrition
Approximately 520 kcal per serving.
