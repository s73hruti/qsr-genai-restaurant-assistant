# Loyalty program tiers

## Earning points
Rewards members earn 1 point per dollar spent. Silver is the starting tier.

## Tier thresholds
Gold starts at 500 points in a calendar year and Platinum at 1500 points. Gold and Platinum members get early access to limited-time offers and double points on weekends.
