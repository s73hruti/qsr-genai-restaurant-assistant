# Allergens and dietary needs

## Where to find allergen details
Allergen details are listed on every menu item page. Our kitchens handle gluten, milk, egg and fish so we cannot guarantee any item is completely allergen free.

## Shared equipment
Vegetarian items are cooked on shared equipment with meat products. No menu item is certified vegan or halal.
