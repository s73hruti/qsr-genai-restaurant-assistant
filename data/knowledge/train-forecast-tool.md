# Using the demand forecast and inventory plan

## Reading the forecast
The forecast shows expected orders per day for a store. The inventory plan converts expected orders into ingredient quantities using the recipe bill of materials.

## Safety stock
Recommended order quantities include safety stock sized for a 95 percent service level. Re-run the forecast every Monday and after any local event.
