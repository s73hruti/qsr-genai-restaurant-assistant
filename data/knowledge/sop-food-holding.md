# Food safety holding times

## Hot holding
Fries may be held for a maximum of 7 minutes in the holding bin. Cooked beef patties must be used within 20 minutes. Chicken nuggets and chicken fillets may be held for 15 minutes.

## Cold storage
Cold items must be stored below 5 degrees Celsius. Log fridge temperatures every 4 hours.
