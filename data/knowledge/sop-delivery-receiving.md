# Receiving deliveries

## Temperature checks
Frozen goods must be below minus 15 degrees Celsius on arrival; reject them above minus 12 degrees. Chilled goods must be below 5 degrees.

## Paperwork
Sign the delivery note within 30 minutes and report discrepancies to the supplier within 24 hours.
