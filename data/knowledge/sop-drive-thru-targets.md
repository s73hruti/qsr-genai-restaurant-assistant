# Drive-thru service targets

## Targets
The target average drive-thru time is 180 seconds.

## Queue actions
When the queue exceeds 10 vehicles, add one runner to take orders at the lane. Escalate to the area manager if the wait exceeds 8 minutes for 30 minutes.
