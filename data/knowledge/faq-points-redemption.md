# Redeeming loyalty points

## Redemption value
Every 100 points can be exchanged for a 1 dollar reward at checkout. Rewards are applied automatically when you tap "Use points".

## Expiry
Points expire 12 months after the date they were earned. The app shows your next expiry date on the rewards page.
