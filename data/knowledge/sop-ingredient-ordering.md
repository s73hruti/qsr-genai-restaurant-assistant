# Ingredient ordering

## Order cutoff
Orders to the distribution center must be placed by 14:00 for next-day delivery. Review the weekly forecast every Monday.

## Delivery schedule
Frozen items such as potato and nuggets are delivered twice a week, on Tuesday and Friday. Fresh produce arrives daily before 06:00.
