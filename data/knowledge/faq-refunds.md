# Refunds and order problems

## Wrong or missing items
If your order is wrong or missing an item, open the order in the app and tap "Report a problem". We reply within 1 hour during opening times.

## When refunds arrive
Refunds for app payments are returned to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
