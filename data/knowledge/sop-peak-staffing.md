# Staffing at peak

## Peak periods
Peak periods are 8am, 12 to 1pm and 7 to 8pm. Minimum crew at peak is 7 on shift.

## Using the forecast
Use the forecast tool to adjust the roster when expected orders exceed baseline by more than 15 percent.
