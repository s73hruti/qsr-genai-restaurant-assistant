# Crispy Fish Burger

## Description
Crumbed fish fillet with tartare sauce and a slice of cheese.

## Price and availability
The Crispy Fish Burger costs $5.60. Category: burger. Available all day during opening hours.

## Allergens and diet
Allergens: fish, gluten, milk. Contains meat, fish or dairy ingredients. Tags: fish.

## Nutrition
Approximately 480 kcal per serving.
