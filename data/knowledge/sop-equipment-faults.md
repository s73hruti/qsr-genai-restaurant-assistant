# Equipment faults and maintenance

## Priority 1 equipment
Fryer, grill and refrigeration faults are priority 1. Call the maintenance hotline within 15 minutes of discovering the fault; response target is 4 hours.

## Shake machine
If the shake machine is down for more than 2 hours log a ticket in the facilities portal.
